Write a handoff document for this room's conversation. This will be injected as context into your next fresh session in this room after a clear.

Include:
- What was being worked on or discussed
- Current state and any open threads
- Key decisions made
- Anything the next session needs to know to pick up smoothly

Be concise but complete. Write in markdown. Do not use any tools. Respond with the handoff text directly.
